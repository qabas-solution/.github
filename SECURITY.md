# Security Policy

## Reporting a problem

Please do not open a public issue for a security problem.

Report it privately to the Team Lead, or by email to `qabas.ai.team@gmail.com`. Include:

- What the problem is
- How to reproduce it
- What the impact could be

We will respond as soon as we can.

## If you leaked a secret

If a password, key, or token was committed or posted by mistake:

1. Tell the Team Lead immediately.
2. Rotate or revoke the secret.
3. Do not rely on deleting the commit or message. Assume the secret is exposed.
